# Superstore-Sales-Analysis---Power-Bi
This Power BI Sales Dashboard provides a visual summary of key business performance metrics. It enables users to monitor sales, profit, shipping costs, and order quantities, while breaking down performance by various dimensions such as region, category, segment, and shipping method.

IT INCLUDES:

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Sales:** 12.64M  
  Represents the total revenue generated across all transactions.

- **Total Profit:** 1.47M  
  Displays the overall profit after deducting costs.

- **Total Quantity Sold:** 178K  
  Indicates the number of units sold.

- **Total Shipping Cost:** 1.35M  
  Shows the total cost incurred for shipping products.

---

## Profit by Region and Segment

A bar chart that displays the **sum of profit by geographic region**, further broken down by **customer segment** (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office).  
This allows a deep dive into how different segments perform across regions.

---

## Sales by Country

A donut chart showing **sales distribution by country**.  
The **United States** leads significantly (42.46%), followed by **Germany**, **Australia**, **France**, and **China**, revealing market dominance and regional focus.

---

## Sales by State

A treemap that visualizes sales at a **state or province level**, with states like **England**, **California**, and **Ile-de-France** showing strong performance.  
This offers localized insights for targeted strategies.

---

## Sales by Category

A bar chart that breaks down total sales across **product categories**:
- **Technology**
- **Furniture**
- **Office Supplies**  
Useful for identifying top-selling product lines and prioritizing inventory.

---

## Sales by Market

A donut chart that analyzes **sales contribution by global market**, including:
- APAC
- US
- EU
- LATAM
- EMEA
- Africa
- Canada  
This helps in assessing market expansion and performance.

---

## Sales by Ship Mode

A horizontal bar chart illustrating sales volume by **shipping mode**:
- **Standard Class** is the most used,
- Followed by **Second Class**, **First Class**, and **Same Day** delivery.  

  Provides insights for optimizing logistics and shipping strategies.

---

## Filters

The dashboard also includes slicers to filter data by:
- **Category**
- **Market**  
These interactive filters enhance user-driven analysis for more focused insights.

---

## Purpose

This dashboard serves as a strategic tool for:
- Tracking overall business performance
- Identifying high-value regions and products
- Improving logistics and customer experience
- Supporting data-driven decision making
